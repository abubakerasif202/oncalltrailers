Safety & Compliance

The 60-Second Hitching Checklist

Safety is our primary priority. Before towing onto public Sydney roads, take one minute to verify these essential hitching points:

✓

50mm Coupling Lock

Ensure the ball coupling is fully seated on your 50mm towball and the release trigger has dropped securely into the locked position.

✓

Dual Safety Chains

Cross the twin ADR-rated safety chains underneath the drawbar and fasten tightly with rated D-shackles to your vehicle's towbar hitch points.

✓

Electrical Connection

Plug the electrical lead into your car's socket. Test brake lights, hazard lights, indicators, and tail running lights before departure.

✓

Jockey Wheel Stowed

Wind the heavy-duty swing-up jockey wheel fully up, pull the release pin, rotate to horizontal transport position, and lock firmly.

✓

60/40 Load Balance

Distribute approximately 60% of your total load weight forward of the tandem axle assembly to maintain positive downforce and stop trailer sway.

✓

Gate Latches Pinned

Close the drop-down rear loading ramp gate and lock both spring-loaded slide bolts with the safety linchpins before driving.
